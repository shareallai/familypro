If a comparison still tells you to choose between X Premium, SuperGrok Lite, SuperGrok and Heavy, check its date before paying. xAI [introduced The X Subscription](https://x.ai/news/x-subscription) on **October 12, 2026**, bringing Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X benefits into one subscription.

**Facts checked: 2026-10-15. USD list prices are for reference only and exclude applicable taxes. Regional prices, offers and eligibility depend on checkout.** This guide explains the change in names and purchase logic. See the [new Grok subscription guide](../xai-grok-x-subscription-plan-guide/) for annual prices, community reactions and migration details.

## 1. Which plans should new customers compare?

The [official pricing documentation](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/pricing) lists Free, Plus at $8/month, Premium at $30/month, Super at $100/month and Ultra at $200/month. Eligible Grok usage across Grok and Cursor shares one weekly pool.

Connect a personal X account to receive X benefits: Plus includes a checkmark, creator tools and fewer ads; Premium and above include full X Premium+ benefits. Buying separate X and Grok subscriptions is no longer the main comparison for new customers.

## 2. What do the old names mean?

| Name on a bill or older guide | Subscription family | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| X Basic, Premium, Premium+ | Legacy X plans | New Premium is a different subscription |
| SuperGrok Lite, SuperGrok | Legacy standalone Grok | Check the existing renewal price and features |
| SuperGrok Plus | Another legacy Grok tier | It is not the new Plus |
| SuperGrok Heavy | Legacy high-tier Grok | Do not assume it is identical to Ultra |
| Free, Plus, Premium, Super, Ultra | The X Subscription | The new unified subscription family |

The launch describes Premium as combining SuperGrok, Cursor Pro and X Premium+ benefits. This does not add together every allowance from the three older products, particularly third-party model usage in Cursor.

## 3. Do SuperGrok and Heavy subscribers need to switch?

**Not currently.** [Switching is optional](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/do-i-have-to-switch); existing plans continue unless changed or cancelled. The announcement also says existing prices and features can be kept.

If Grok alone meets your needs, keeping the current plan is reasonable. If you pay separately for several products, compare the new quote against your actual combined bill. Heavy users should verify the particular models, features and discounts they rely on: the public information does not establish full equivalence with Ultra. Current retention rules are not a permanent price guarantee.

## 4. A shared pool is not a fixed message count

The [weekly usage rules](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/usage-limits) cover eligible first-party usage for the same paid seat across Grok and Cursor. Different tasks consume different amounts. Premium's 4x, Super's 15x and Ultra's 50x Plus usage cannot be converted into a guaranteed daily message or video count.

A large Cursor coding task leaves less allowance for other Grok tasks that week. Grok Bot is included from Premium and uses that same pool. Cursor third-party models cost extra from first use. Grok API access also comes with separately billed calls at standard model rates, outside the subscription pool.

## 5. Check the accounts and billing channel before switching

Record the existing renewal price, expiry date and purchase channel before following the [account unification process](https://docs.x.ai/x-subscription/unify-accounts). Confirm the exact Grok, Cursor and personal X accounts, especially when work and personal accounts are separate.

**Completed unification cannot be undone, including by Support.** Eligible old subscriptions receive a prorated refund, but Apple-billed Cursor must be cancelled separately through Apple. For Google Play subscriptions, check whether renewal remains enabled.

Use the [new plan and migration guide](../xai-grok-x-subscription-plan-guide/) to compare a specific offer with your existing workflow before committing.
