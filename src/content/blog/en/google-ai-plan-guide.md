Google AI is sold as `Google AI Plus`, `Google AI Pro`, and `Google AI Ultra`. Comparing the plans by an old daily-prompt table is no longer reliable: Gemini, Flow, Search AI Mode, and developer products vary by model, feature, account, and region.

This guide reflects Google's public US plan page as checked on **October 14, 2026**. Prices, trials, purchasable tiers, and feature availability vary by account and country. Treat your own Google One checkout page as the source of truth.

## 1. Plan positioning

- **Google AI Plus** is for regular Gemini, Gemini Notebook, and light creative use. It includes 400 GB or 2 TB of storage, 2x Gemini usage relative to non-AI subscribers, and entry-level Flow credits.
- **Google AI Pro** is the working tier for research, creation, and development. It includes 5 TB, 4x Gemini usage, and broader access to Flow, Search AI Mode, Jules, Antigravity, AI Studio, and Gemini in Google apps.
- **Google AI Ultra** is for sustained high-volume generation, Deep Think, or higher agent-development limits. It comes in 20 TB and 30 TB tiers with 5x or 20x Pro Gemini usage.

For occasional writing, research, and image work, Plus is usually sufficient. Pro is the more appropriate choice when Gemini Notebook, Flow, or coding agents are part of a daily workflow. Ultra is justified by a demonstrated need for its higher limits or exclusive features.

## 2. Published plan limits

### 2.1 Core comparison

| Plan | Storage | Gemini usage | Google Flow credits | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google AI Plus | 400 GB or 2 TB | 2x non-AI usage | 200/month | Study, personal research, light creation |
| Google AI Pro | 5 TB | 4x non-AI usage | 1,000/month | Frequent research, creative work, development |
| Google AI Ultra 5x | 20 TB | 5x Pro usage | 10,000/month | High-volume video and agent workflows |
| Google AI Ultra 20x | 30 TB | 20x Pro usage | 25,000/month | Very high-volume generation and development |

The Plus card still shows 400 GB, while Google's detailed comparison lists 400 GB or 2 TB. Check your own Google One checkout for the available storage option, price, and included benefits; do not assume the two Plus options are identical.

Flow Music has separate monthly credits: 3,000, 10,000, 30,000, and 30,000 respectively. Do not combine those credits with Flow video credits.

### 2.2 Dynamic rather than fixed Gemini quotas

Google presents Gemini access as relative usage limits, not a universal number of messages per day. Prompt complexity, model choice, attachments, and conversation length affect consumption. Pro and both Ultra tiers list a **1 million token** context window; that matters more than a prompt count for large documents, research collections, and codebases.

### 2.3 Pricing and regional availability

Google can show different prices, trials, and tiers by country and account. Many products described below are US-only, English-only, device-dependent, or still rolling out. Do not use a price from another country, or an old screenshot, as a checkout quote.

## 3. Google AI products and their limits

### 3.1 Gemini app

Plus currently lists Gemini 3.6 Flash. Pro and Ultra add access to Gemini 4 Argon in eligible countries, alongside listed Gemini 3.1 Pro and Deep Research access. Context windows are 128k tokens for Plus and 1 million for Pro/Ultra; usage multipliers do not translate into fixed message counts.

Gemini is the main interface for chat, files, Deep Research, and image, video, and music generation. The published 2x, 4x, 5x, and 20x figures are relative access levels, not fixed message quotas. Pro and Ultra add a 1 million token context window; Ultra also includes Deep Think. Gemini Agent is currently listed for US English users.

### 3.2 Gemini Notebook

Google lists Gemini Notebook as the source-based research product, with the standalone entry point listed at [notebook.google](https://notebook.google/). It is suited to project documents, course materials, web sources, and meeting records. The overview page describes limits as More, Expanded, Higher, and Highest, but does not publish one universal count for notebooks, sources, or generations.

### 3.3 Google Flow and Google Flow Music

Flow is Google's video-creation product. Its published credit tiers are 200, 1,000, 10,000, and 25,000 per month; higher tiers also list 4K upscaling and custom Flow tools. Flow Music is separate, with 3,000, 10,000, 30,000, and 30,000 monthly credits. Test a representative project before choosing a tier, because model and output settings affect credit use.

### 3.4 Search AI Mode, Deep Search, and Google Finance

Pro and Ultra include higher access to capable Gemini models, image generation, agentic features, and Deep Search in Google Search. Google marks AI Mode agentic features and Deep Search as US-only. Google Finance Deep Search is separately listed with More access on Plus and higher levels above it; it is currently US-only, and fixed query counts are not published on the plan overview.

### 3.5 Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Vids, and Meet

Google lists Gmail proofreading, Vids generation help, and Meet note-taking among its in-app Gemini benefits. Gmail AI Overview is US-only and some Meet features are English-only. The overview does not give these actions separate numerical quotas.

### 3.6 AI Studio, Jules, Antigravity, and Android Studio

AI Studio gives developers increasing access to capable models, the Vibe Coding agent, Nano Banana Pro image features, and early features. Jules handles repository tasks; the plan table grades task limits, concurrency, and model access from Limited to Highest without publishing fixed counts. Antigravity agent requests and Android Studio agent assistance follow the same tiered pattern. Google Developer Program premium includes monthly Cloud credits of $10 on Pro, $40 on Ultra 5x, and $100 on Ultra 20x.

### 3.7 Tasks in Gemini, Daily Brief, and Chrome auto browse

[Tasks in Gemini](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094507) handles multi-step tasks and ongoing workflows for eligible Pro/Ultra personal accounts. It requires age 18+, Keep Activity, and Personal Intelligence. The help page lists availability wherever Gemini Apps are supported except the EEA, Nigeria, Switzerland, and the UK. Daily Brief remains marked US-only on the plan page.

Chrome auto browse is listed for Pro and Ultra in the US and India. Its access levels are Expanded, Higher, and Highest, without a fixed task count on the plan overview.

### 3.8 Google Photos, Project Genie, and Playground

Google Photos includes Remix and photo-to-video access that rises from More on Plus to Highest on Ultra. Some photo features are US-only, and the overview gives no monthly generation count.

Project Genie remains an Ultra-only interactive-world feature, listed in more than 140 countries without a universal quota on the plan overview. Playground creates custom games and is currently US-only, with access levels rising across plans; it is not an Ultra-exclusive benefit.

### 3.9 Google Home, Health, YouTube, storage, and family sharing

Pro includes Google Home Premium Standard with 30 days of event history; Ultra includes Advanced with 24/7 event history and event descriptions. Google Health Premium requires the Google Health app plus a compatible Pixel Watch or Fitbit and is region-limited. In eligible countries, Pro includes YouTube Premium Lite and Ultra includes YouTube Premium Individual. Storage is 400 GB or 2 TB, 5 TB, 20 TB, or 30 TB across Gmail, Drive, and Photos, with family sharing for up to five people.

Family sharing covers storage and select AI benefits for the plan manager and up to five other members. It does not cover every bundled subscription. Google's [Pro benefits guide](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/14534406) states that YouTube Premium Lite is for the manager only, Google Developer Program premium cannot be shared, and Colab's 200 CCUs are manager-only and exclude trial memberships. Calculate those personal benefits separately from family value.

[AI credits](https://support.google.com/googleone/answer/16287445?hl=en) for eligible features are pooled across the family group, rather than duplicated for every member. Pro/Ultra managers can buy extra credits for Flow and Antigravity in supported regions. These AI credits are unavailable in Japan; subscriptions billed through a third party, Pixel Pass, or Apple are ineligible for top-ups. Flow Music credits remain separate.

## 4. Choosing a tier by workload

### 4.1 Plus

Plus suits regular writing, learning, personal research, occasional Flow work, and a genuine need for 400 GB or 2 TB of storage. It is not a substitute for a high-volume video or software-agent plan.

### 4.2 Pro

Pro is the balanced option for frequent source-based research, Google Workspace use, Flow projects, and development work involving Jules, Antigravity, or AI Studio. Its 5 TB storage, 4x Gemini usage, and 1,000 Flow credits are the clearest measurable upgrades.

### 4.3 Ultra

Choose Ultra only when monthly Flow demand, ongoing agent work, Deep Think, or 20 TB/30 TB storage has a clear business or personal value. Project Genie, YouTube Premium, and Google Home benefits remain regional or device-dependent additions.

## 5. What to calculate before subscribing

| Need | Record | Compare with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long Gemini tasks | Document size, weekly frequency, need for persistent context | Pro/Ultra 1M-token context; Gemini has no fixed published message count |
| Flow video | Credits used by 10 representative projects and expected monthly volume | 200 / 1,000 / 10,000 / 25,000 Flow credits per month; keep a 20–30% buffer |
| Flow Music | Credits per finished track and monthly output | 3,000 / 10,000 / 30,000 / 30,000 separate music credits |
| Notebook research | Concurrent notebooks, sources per project, weekly outputs | No universal public count; upgrade only for a measurable workload |
| Coding agents | Jules tasks, concurrency, AI Studio/Antigravity use | Fixed public task limits are not listed on the overview page |
| Storage | Current use plus 12-month growth plus 20% buffer | 400 GB or 2 TB / 5 TB / 20 TB / 30 TB |

Confirm availability inside the same Google account before paying. Features absent from the account should not be treated as plan value. If you value YouTube, Home, Health, Tasks in Gemini, Deep Search, auto browse, or photo generation, verify its country, language, and device conditions separately.
